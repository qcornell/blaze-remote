Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Blaze is a remote-control app for Roku and Amazon Fire TV devices. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Blaze does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Blaze has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

How the app communicates

Blaze communicates directly with the Roku and Fire TV devices on your own Wi-Fi network in order to find them and send remote-control commands (button presses and text you type into a TV search box). These messages travel only over your local network, between your phone and your TV, and are never sent to us.

To work out which Wi-Fi network your phone is on, the app may briefly open a connection to a public internet address and immediately close it. No data is sent over that connection.

Information stored on your device

So it can reconnect quickly, the app stores the following only on your phone:

  • The names and local network addresses of TVs you have connected to
  • Whether you prefer Roku or Fire TV
  • A randomly generated identifier and security key the app uses to pair with your Fire TV

This information never leaves your device except to talk to your own TV. Deleting the app removes it.

Local Network permission

iOS asks for permission to access devices on your local network. Blaze needs this permission to find and control your TV. It is used for nothing else.

Children

Blaze does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Support

The app can't find my TV

  • Make sure your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network).
  • Make sure the TV is on and awake, with its home screen showing.
  • On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Blaze is turned on.
  • You can connect directly with Enter IP manually. Find the IP on a Roku under Settings → Network → About, or on a Fire TV under Settings → My Fire TV → About → Network.

Roku connects but buttons do nothing

On the Roku, go to Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Default or Enabled.

Fire TV pairing

When you tap your Fire TV, a PIN appears on the TV screen. Type it into the app. If no PIN appears, wake the TV with its own remote and try again.

Volume doesn't work

Volume and power control work on TVs with Roku or Fire TV built in. On a streaming stick plugged into a regular TV, use the TV's own remote for volume.
